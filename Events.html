Community Outreach

Overview

At Nyumbani Senior Centre, we are committed to making a positive impact in the local community by reaching out to seniors in need. Our community outreach efforts are aimed at providing support, resources, and companionship to seniors, as well as fostering a sense of belonging and connection within the community.

Events

  • Senior Health Fair: Saturday, April 20th, 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM
  • Community Bingo Night: Friday, May 3rd, 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM

Partnerships

We are proud to collaborate with the following organizations to support our community outreach efforts:

  • The Nzembe Foundation
  • T.S.S Transportation Services

Initiatives

Our outreach initiatives include:

  • Monthly food drives to support seniors facing food insecurity
  • Weekly wellness workshops covering topics such as nutrition and exercise

Get Involved

Interested in supporting our community outreach efforts? Here are some ways you can get involved:

  • Volunteer at one of our upcoming events
  • Donate to our food drive or wellness program
